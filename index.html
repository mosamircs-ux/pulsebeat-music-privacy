Privacy Policy

App: PulseBeat Music (package com.pulsebeat.musicplayer)
Effective date: 19 November 2026
Contact: mosamircs12101996@gmail.com

This policy explains what information PulseBeat Music handles and why. It is based on how the app works and the permissions it requests.

1. Summary

  • PulseBeat Music plays audio files stored on your device. It does not require an account.
  • The developer does not receive your music library, playlists, or listening history.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use advertising identifiers and other data as described in its own policies.

2. Information the app handles on your device

  • Music library: the app reads audio files on your device to build your library, show metadata, and play tracks.
  • Playlists and settings: playlists, track labels, excluded folders, equalizer settings, sleep timer, and interface preferences are stored on your device.
  • Track information: if you edit track information in the app, the change is saved to the file or the app's database on your device.
  • Notifications: the app shows playback controls in the notification area.

3. Information shared with third parties

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device and usage information, including an advertising ID (the Android advertising identifier), IP address, device model, and ad interaction data. This is used to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads). Google's handling of data is described in the Google privacy policy.

The app does not send your music library, playlists, or listening history to the developer or to advertisers.

5. Data retention and deletion

The app keeps its data on your device. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. Your music files are not deleted by the app when you remove a playlist.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Your data stays on your device, protected by your device's security settings. No method of storage is completely secure.
